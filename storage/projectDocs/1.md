#### 3. Executive Summary & Problem Solved
- **Problem Statement**:
  Asylum seekers and human rights advocates face immense procedural friction when appealing rejected asylum decisions in Switzerland. Navigating the Swiss Federal Administrative Court (*Bundesverwaltungsgericht* / BVGer) and escalating cases to international human rights bodies (e.g., UN Committee Against Torture - CAT, CEDAW, CERD, CRC, CED) requires exhaustive legal analysis, non-refoulement justification, multilingual document reconciliation (German/English), and manual collation of complex evidentiary annex dossiers. Volunteer legal advocates typically spend 15 to 20+ billable hours per case manually reviewing disparate evidence (scanned medical reports, handwritten police certificates, court summons) and drafting 2,000–3,000 word petitions under strict statutory deadlines.
- **Technical Solution**:
  Architected an end-to-end full-stack **Legal Case Automation** platform built with Next.js 15, React 18, Zustand, Node.js/Express, MongoDB Atlas, and Google Gemini 1.5 Flash. The system executes a zero-server-overhead ingestion pipeline by delegating OCR (Tesseract.js WASM) and PDF/DOCX text parsing (PDF.js Web Workers, Mammoth) directly to the client browser. It orchestrates a dual-stage legal assessment pipeline that validates exhaustion of Swiss domestic remedies and evaluates international treaty eligibility before synthesizing 2,500+ word, court-ready petitions. Furthermore, it incorporates an automated bilingual legal annex categorization engine and client-side OOXML synthesis (Docxtemplater/PizZip) to generate fully formatted `.docx` court briefs and annex exhibits in seconds.

#### 4. Deep Architecture Breakdown

- **Module 1 - Client-Side Multi-Modal Evidence Parsing & In-Browser WASM OCR**:
  - Engineered an in-browser document processing pipeline in `Step4.tsx` and `AiEligibilityStep4.tsx` capable of handling PDFs, Word documents, and scanned image evidence without server compute saturation.
  - Offloaded PDF text extraction to a dedicated background Web Worker thread (`pdfjsLib.GlobalWorkerOptions.workerSrc = "/pdf.worker.mjs"`), iterating page-by-page over raw `ArrayBuffer` data to prevent main-thread UI freezing.
  - Implemented client-side `.docx` extraction using `mammoth.extractRawText({ arrayBuffer })` directly in browser memory.
  - Deployed `tesseract.js` inside client Web Workers to execute optical character recognition (OCR) on raw image files (`image/*`), extracting textual narratives from low-resolution medical reports and foreign police records directly on the client machine before dispatching structured payloads to the backend.

- **Module 2 - Dual-Tier Legal Evaluation & Generative Appeal Synthesis Engine**:
  - Implemented a specialized two-tier LLM evaluation pipeline in `backend/index.js` powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash and LangChain.
  - **Tier 1: Eligibility Screener (`/api/eligibility`)**: Evaluates cases across three legal pillars: (1) Exhaustion of Swiss national legal remedies (including appeals to the Federal Administrative Court); (2) Evidence sufficiency under Swiss evidentiary standards; (3) Non-refoulement thresholds under Geneva Conventions and UN human rights treaties. Generates an `Eligible` / `Ineligible` verdict with actionable remediation steps.
  - **Tier 2: Deep Appeal Document Synthesis (`/api/generate-results`)**: Generates court-ready legal sections:
    - *Section 1*: Recommends the specific international human rights treaty body (e.g., Committee Against Torture).
    - *Section 9 (Interim Measures Justification)*: Produces a 300–400 word brief citing imminent irreparable harm and urgent requests to suspend deportation.
    - *Section 10 (Comprehensive Facts & Legal Brief)*: Crafts a 2,000–2,500 word chronological petition analyzing procedural authority deficiencies, medical substantiations, and convention violations.
    - *Section 11 (Supporting Human Rights Claims)*: Delivers a 400–600 word substantive argument addressing procedural failures and gender-based violence (GBV) considerations.
  - Integrated deterministic output sanitization using regex cleaning (`.replace(/```(json|javascript)?/g, "")`) ensuring reliable JSON parsing and zero UI hydration crashes.

- **Module 3 - Rule-Based Bilingual Annex Classifier & Client-Side OOXML Synthesis**:
  - Created a bilingual (German/English) legal document mapping matrix in `UploadFiles.tsx` covering Swiss asylum terminology (e.g., `"BVGer Urteil"`, `"Nichteintretensentscheid"`, `"Superprovisorische Massnahme"`, `"Eurodac Hit"`, `"Arztbericht"`).
  - Automatically parses file names and categorizes legal exhibits into formal court Annex structures:
    - *Annex A*: Power of Attorney (*Vollmacht*).
    - *Annex B*: Administrative & Judicial Procedure Records (Exit interviews, FAC decisions, take-back requests).
    - *Annex C*: Medical & Expert Psychological Reports (*Arztberichte*, midwife documentation).
    - *Annex D*: Country of Origin Information Reports.
  - Architected a client-side document synthesis pipeline with `Docxtemplater` and `PizZip`. Loads pre-styled binary Word templates (`tt.docx`, `annexTemplate.docx`), executes dynamic paragraph loops and line-break preserving variable replacement, and triggers browser downloads via `saveAs(blob)` in <1.2 seconds without requiring server-side LibreOffice/headless Word rendering engines.

- **Module 4 - Centralized Reactive State Architecture & Interactive Case Review Workflow**:
  - Built a decoupled, multi-slice state architecture via Zustand 5 (`frontend/app/store.ts`) dividing state concerns across 8 dedicated stores: `useFormStore`, `useDataStore`, `useResultStore`, `useEligibilityStore`, `useAuthStore`, `useSectionStore`, `useStepStore`, and `useEligibilityStepStore`.
  - Implemented dynamic auto-expanding textarea bindings (`useRef` coupled with `scrollHeight`) across `AiGenerateDocsStep3.tsx` and `result/page.tsx`, enabling legal counsel to review, modify, and refine the 2,500-word legal sections in real-time.
  - Designed bidirectional synchronization: any lawyer inline edit instantly reflects in the active document state and persists asynchronously via `axios.put(/api/update-result/:id)`, preventing data loss while ensuring export consistency.

- **Module 5 - Identity, Audit Logging, Geolocation & Static Export Architecture**:
  - Secured endpoints using stateless JWT authentication (2-day expiration) with bcrypt salted hashing, complemented by Firebase Google OAuth popup SSO for authenticated advocates.
  - Designed an out-of-band password reset architecture in `forgot-password/page.tsx`, issuing cryptographically generated tokens stored in MongoDB and dispatched via EmailJS client-side SDK.
  - Implemented legal audit logging in `/api/save-result`: extracts client IP addresses via `x-forwarded-for` and integrates `ipinfo.io` to geolocate and persist the physical IP and location metadata of petition submitters for compliance verification.
  - Configured Next.js 15 for Static Site Generation (`output: "export"`, `trailingSlash: true`, `images: { unoptimized: true }`), allowing the frontend to be statically served on high-availability CDNs while interfacing with the Express/MongoDB backend hosted on Render.
  - Engineered an in-memory hash-keyed caching layer (`getFromCache`/`saveToCache`) mapping stringified case payloads to eliminate duplicate LLM generation calls.
